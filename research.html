Research Explained

Academic papers are necessarily compact and specialized. Important engineering ideas can therefore be difficult to see behind the equations, methodology, and technical details.

Research Explained revisits published research and ongoing research directions from a broader perspective: the engineering problem, the underlying physical or conceptual idea, the method used to address it, and what the results mean in practice.

The articles are organized around four research directions.
