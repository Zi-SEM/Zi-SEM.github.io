Computational Mechanics & Fracture

This research area covers computational fracture and damage mechanics, XFEM, XEFG, meshfree methods, multiple-crack representation, alternative fracture descriptions, and multiscale modeling.

The recurring question is how a numerical representation can capture the essential mechanics without forcing the physics to conform to the discretization.
